# Fire safety in flats - a guide for tenants

- Keep corridors and stairs clear: no bikes, prams or bags.
- Fire doors must close by themselves. Report a damaged fire door as a repair straight away.
- Test your smoke alarm every month.
- If there is a fire in your flat, get out, close the door and call 999.
- If the fire is elsewhere in the building, follow the instructions on the notice in your hallway.
