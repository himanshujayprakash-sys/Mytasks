# Gas safety programme 2026-27

- Every home with a gas appliance gets an annual safety check before the anniversary of the last one.
- Servicing contractor: renewed in October 2026 for 2027 to 2029 (signed agreement in the corporate files).
- Access procedure: three letters, a visit by the housing officer, then legal action as a last resort.
- Compliance at the end of October 2026: 99.8 per cent; three homes in the access procedure.
- Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms are tested at every annual check.
