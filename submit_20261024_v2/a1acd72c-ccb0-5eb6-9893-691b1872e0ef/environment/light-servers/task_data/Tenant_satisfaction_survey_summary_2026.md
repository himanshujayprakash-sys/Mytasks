# Tenant satisfaction survey 2026 - summary

- Overall satisfaction: 78 per cent (last year 76).
- Satisfaction with repairs: 74 per cent.
- Time taken to complete the most recent repair: 69 per cent satisfied.
- Treated fairly and with respect: 84 per cent.
- Complaint handling: 41 per cent satisfied; an improvement plan is in place.

Survey carried out by telephone in June 2026 with 812 tenants.
