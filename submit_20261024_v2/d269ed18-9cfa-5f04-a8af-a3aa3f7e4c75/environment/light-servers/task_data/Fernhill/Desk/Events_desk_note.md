# Events desk note (winter 2026)

Left at the desk by Bronwen Teague, revised 30 November 2026.

## Where things come in
- Guests message the guest WhatsApp line about directions, parking and their own details.
- Guests reply to the RSVP text line, +441632961188, which sends the RSVP reminders. The desk line, +441632961100, is for calls and viewings.
- Couples write to the events mailbox, events@fernhillbarn.example, or on their conversation in the Fernhill messenger. Hattie records what she agrees with a
  couple by phone as an internal note on that conversation; the couple do not see notes.
- Voicemails left on the events desk phone are typed up by whoever is on the desk into the voicemail transcripts file in the Desk folder.
- Hattie also keeps notes of phone calls on each couple's contact in the CRM.
- Couples keep their own guest list (who is invited to the day or only the evening, and each guest's age group) in the planning portal, which
  writes straight into the guests table of the records system, at any hour and without telling the desk. A portal change is a written change
  from the couple and counts from the moment it is in the table: final numbers go out on the guest list as it stands when they are sent, and
  anything worked from an earlier version of a guest's row is put right first.
- Chefs accept or decline their shifts on the kitchen shift calendar. The kitchen rota is printed on the 1st of the month and is not reprinted.

## Where things go out
- Final numbers go to the couple as one message on their messenger conversation.
- The balance invoice is raised in the accounts system against the couple's customer record.
- Per-event supplier orders go from the kitchen mailbox, kitchen@fernhillbarn.example.
