# Supplier ordering standard (kitchen)

- The events desk orders the portion-priced items for each event: beef from Hollybank Butchers, fish from Severn & Wye Fish and certified
  sealed meals from Allerfree Kitchens. Dairy, bakery and vegetables are standing weekly orders and are never ordered per event.
- Beef: the production sheet's MB and MBA portions. Fish: the MF and MFA portions.
- Order the production sheet portions plus 4%, rounded up to a whole portion. Certified sealed meals are ordered at the exact number.
- Name the item as the supplier sheet for that week names it.
- Orders go from the kitchen mailbox, one email per supplier, for delivery on the day before the event.
