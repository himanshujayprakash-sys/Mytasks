Sources behind the Northlight Summit papers (task 04r)

The summit, its hotels, staff, speakers, bookings, rates and standards are made up. Two public documents shaped how the
statement standard and the visitor levy are laid out; they are reused under the Open Government Licence (version 3) and
nothing in them is quoted as evidence.

- Scottish Government, "Visitor Levy (Scotland) Act 2024: guidance for accommodation providers"
  https://www.gov.scot/publications/visitor-levy-scotland-act-2024-guidance/  (revised July 2026; consulted 22 Oct 2026)
- HM Revenue and Customs, "Hotels, holiday accommodation and VAT (VAT Notice 709/3)"
  https://www.gov.uk/guidance/hotels-holiday-accommodation-and-vat-notice-7093  (revised June 2026; consulted 22 Oct 2026)
