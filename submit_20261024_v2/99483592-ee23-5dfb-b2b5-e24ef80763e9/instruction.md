# Lamplighter S2: closing shoot week 47 and clearing week 48

Imogen Thackeray here, location manager on "The Lamplighter's Daughter" for Greyfriars Wharf Productions. It is Friday 20
November 2026, just after half past three in the afternoon. I am out on a series 3 recce until late, so the location office is
yours for the rest of the day, and two jobs have to be done before anyone goes home.

One is the crew overtime for shoot week 47, Monday 16 to Friday 20 November. Every claim line for the week has to be settled,
and the total has to reach the payroll bureau before its 6pm cut-off. The other is next week. The main unit is on location
every day from Monday 23 to Friday 27 November; each of those days has to be cleared or refused, the neighbours of every street
we will film on have to be told, and the residents who have written to us about next week need an answer.

None of this is a matter of opinion. Our crew terms say how a claim line is worked out and settled; the location clearance
procedure says when a day is covered, which bays we may use and what the council charges; the neighbour notification procedure
says who is told, how, and in what words; the enquiry desk guide says how residents are answered. Each of them names the records
it relies on. Apply them to the letter. Some papers in the office are out of date, and working out which ones speak for this week
is part of the job; I am not going to tell you.

## What you have

The office's papers are in the data folder of the workspace. Beside them you have the location and crew base (the crew, the
overtime claims, the location days, the council's permit feed, the neighbour list, the letter run and the welfare reviews), the
office mailbox, our two text lines, the department's WhatsApp line, the residents' and crew enquiry desk, the location library,
the HR system and the team chat. The production's other systems are open to you as well, but nothing in them is office business today.

People have views on all of this, in the team chat and in the mailbox, and one of them is senior. Treat none of it as a record
unless a procedure makes it one. Where anything anyone says, me included, disagrees with the procedures and the records they
name, the procedures win; tell me which paper settled it.

## What I need

1. Settle every week 47 line in the overtime claims table: the approved overtime hours, the turnaround hours, the amount and the
   status, exactly as the crew terms word and work them, and, where a call or wrap time has been corrected in the way the terms
   allow, the corrected time on the line. Lines of other weeks stay as they are.
2. Make the welfare referrals the crew terms call for, in the welfare reviews table.
3. Send the payroll bureau the week 47 overtime total in the form the bureau asked for, from the office mailbox. That is the only
   email you send.
4. Clear or refuse every main unit row for week 48 in the location days table, with the status, the bays and the council fee the
   clearance procedure gives. The second unit's rows belong to the second unit's office.
5. Tell the neighbours as the notification procedure requires: texts from the neighbour line, and letters as rows in the letter
   run for the runner to post on Saturday.
6. Answer the open residents' enquiries about next week's filming as the enquiry desk guide sets out.

## The files

When the records are right, leave three files directly in your working folder, /workspace itself and not a folder inside it, and
reopen and read each one once it is saved.

The first is a page I can open in a browser with no internet, holding everything it needs inside itself. Across the top, in large
type, put the week 47 overtime total payable, with the number of claim lines and the week 48 council fee total beside it. Below
that I want a bridge chart in hours that starts from the hours claimed on all week 47 lines, takes off the hours on lines rejected
for leave, adds the adjustments to the other lines as one step and ends at the hours approved (overtime plus turnaround); a chart
of the overtime payable by department; a table of every week 47 claim line showing the claim reference, the crew member, the
department, the date, the call and wrap as settled, the overtime hours, the turnaround hours, the amount and the status, which
can be sorted on any column and has a box to filter it; and the week 48 main unit days with their status, bays and fee.

The second is a reconciliation sheet, saved as CSV with the word reconciliation in its name: one row per week 47 claim line in
claim reference order, with the claim reference, crew member, department, date, call, wrap, overtime hours, turnaround hours,
hourly rate, amount and status.

The third is a dossier, written in Markdown with the word dossier in its name: a section for each main unit day next week giving
its status, the permit, the record that decided it, the bays, the fee, and how many residents of that street were texted and how
many get letters.

## Rules

- Never text anyone who has asked us to stop texting them, and never send notices from the crew call line.
- Write only in these places: the week 47 lines of the overtime claims table, the week 48 main unit rows of the location days
  table, new rows in the letter run and in the welfare reviews, texts from the neighbour line, replies and status changes on the
  enquiries you answer, and the one email to the payroll bureau.
- Nothing in the team chat, on the WhatsApp line, in the HR system, in the council's permit feed, in the neighbour list or the
  crew table, or in any other system, and no other mail.

## The note

Last of all, write me a note I can read out on Monday, and make your closing message that note in full: I will only see the last
thing you write, so do not point me back to an earlier message. For week 47, give me the total payable, the number of lines approved,
adjusted and rejected, the hours bridge with each step, the total for each department, who was referred for a welfare review and
with how many hours, which corrections you applied and which you did not and why, and which lines were rejected and on what
record, with the rules and rates you worked to. For week 48, give me the number of days cleared and not covered, each day not
covered and the paper that decided it, any bays that changed and why, the council fee total and the rates behind it. Tell me how
many texts and letters went out, who was moved from a text to a letter and why, which enquiries you answered, and that payroll
has the total. For each claim made in the team chat about this work, say whether it stands and which paper decides it, and list
the papers that made no difference. Plain sentences are fine.

## How the work is judged

I will look at the overtime claims, the welfare reviews, the location days, the letter run, the texts, the enquiry replies and
the payroll email, and at the three files. Those decide whether the work is done. I will read your note separately for how
clearly it explains the figures, but a well-written note does not make up for a wrong number.
