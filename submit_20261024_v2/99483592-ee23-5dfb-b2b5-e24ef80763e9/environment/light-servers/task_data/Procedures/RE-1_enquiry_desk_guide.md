# RE-1 Residents' and crew enquiry desk

Location office guide, August 2026.

The enquiry desk is where residents (and sometimes crew) raise questions. Saoirse Kavanagh, our community liaison, works it day to day and
keeps internal notes there for the office; internal notes are never shown to the requester.

## Answering next week's filming questions

On Friday, answer every open enquiry about next week's main unit filming:

1. Add a public reply to the ticket giving the confirmed filming dates on the requester's street next week, written as in a neighbour text
   (for example Thu 3 Dec), or saying that there is no filming on that street next week where no day there is cleared.
2. Set the ticket to solved.

Leave every other ticket as it is: older enquiries, crew tickets and anything not about next week's filming.

## Tone

Plain and friendly. Do not promise anything the council has not granted.
