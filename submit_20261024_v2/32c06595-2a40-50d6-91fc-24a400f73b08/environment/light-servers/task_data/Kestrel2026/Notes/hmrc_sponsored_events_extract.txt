Extract prepared for the events team from GOV.UK guidance on Gift Aid and sponsored events (Open Government Licence v3.0).

Sponsorship payments for a sponsored event can qualify for Gift Aid when they are gifts from individuals who have made a Gift Aid declaration.
Payments do not qualify when the donor gets a benefit in return, for example an auction lot or a raffle ticket.
A participant's own payment towards their own fundraising does not qualify where the participant gets a place or other benefit in return.
Payments from companies cannot carry Gift Aid; companies get relief through corporation tax instead.
A charity must not claim Gift Aid that another body has already claimed on the same payment.
