# Kestrel Coast Marathon 2026: closing out our charity places

Bethan Coleridge here, Events Fundraising Manager at Linnet Lane Hospice. It is Tuesday
10 November 2026. Fundraising for this year's Kestrel Coast Marathon closed at the end of
October and today is close-out day for our fifty charity places. I am at a trustees'
away day until this evening, so I am handing you the whole close-out, start to end.

What each runner owes, what we reclaim in Gift Aid and how we speak to each runner are
settled by the charity place agreement for this race and by our written procedures, not
by me. Follow them exactly, and keep your workings clear enough for a colleague to
retrace. If a paper and a person disagree, whoever the person is, myself included, go
with the paper and name it to me.

The charity place records already carry close values: our data desk keyed a draft from an
export a week ago. Some of those values will be right and some will not. By the end, each
place record must hold the raised total, the Gift Aid, the outcome (in the agreement's own
words) and the amount due that the agreement and the procedures produce, to the penny,
plus the SMS consent our supporter contact policy gives the runner. A record that is
already right stays exactly as it is; rewriting it with identical values counts as a
change. Go through the place records before you alter any of them.

The October Gift Aid claim needs its lines in the claims table, one per runner, as our Gift
Aid procedure describes. Nothing already in that table from earlier claims changes, and
the donations table itself is not ours to edit.

Every amount due gets an invoice from our accounts system to that runner's own account,
made out as the agreement says. Nobody else is invoiced and nobody gets a payment link.

Everything you need is in the office. The folders hold the agreement for this year and
last year's, the close-out, Gift Aid, supporter contact and hardship waiver procedures,
the signed waiver forms, the employers' letters, the paying-in log, the timing company's
results, the data desk's keyed sheet and handover note, and the usual event papers. The
place records, the donations and the claims are in the records system. Runners and donors
reach us through the events mailbox, the events messaging line, the supporter care desk
and our text numbers; staff leave lives in the HR system; the team chat and the accounts
system are open to you as well. The procedures say which of those count for what.

The team has been generous with opinions in the chat, one of them from a senior colleague
who will expect it to be followed. A chat message is only an opinion until a paper backs
it.

When the records, the claim and the invoices are right, tell each runner how their place
closed: exactly one notice per runner, by the route our supporter contact policy gives.
A text goes from the supporter text number; an email goes from the events mailbox with
the subject "Kestrel Coast Marathon 2026: your fundraising close". The body, for each
outcome, is exactly:

- Minimum met: "Linnet Lane Hospice: thank you! Your Kestrel Coast Marathon fundraising closed at £R, reaching your £M minimum. There is nothing more to pay."
- Shortfall invoiced: "Linnet Lane Hospice: your Kestrel Coast Marathon fundraising closed at £R, short of your £M minimum. We are invoicing the £A difference, due by 1 December 2026."
- Shortfall waived: "Linnet Lane Hospice: your Kestrel Coast Marathon fundraising closed at £R. Your hardship waiver means there is nothing to pay."
- Deferred to 2027: "Linnet Lane Hospice: your Kestrel Coast Marathon place is deferred to 2027, and the £R raised so far stays on your page."
- Non-starter - place fee invoiced: "Linnet Lane Hospice: as you did not start the Kestrel Coast Marathon, we are invoicing the £A place fee, due by 1 December 2026."
- Non-starter - no charge: "Linnet Lane Hospice: sorry we missed you at the Kestrel Coast Marathon. There is nothing to pay."

R is the runner's raised total, M their minimum and A the amount due, each written with
two decimal places and a comma for thousands, as in £1,240.00.

After that, put every runner the agreement offers early entry on the 2027 early-entry
list as subscribed. Anyone who has unsubscribed from that list keeps that status; no step
of yours may resubscribe them.

Beyond all that, change nothing. No chat posts, nothing over the messaging line, no other
list, record or account touched, and no runner hears from us twice.

Save three files directly in /workspace (not in a folder inside it): a page, a dossier and
a reconciliation sheet. The page is an HTML file that has to work offline in a browser,
with every script, style and chart embedded in it. Its banner, in big type, gives the team's raised total, and next to it the Gift Aid
we are claiming and how many invoices we raised and for how much. Under the banner: a
waterfall chart running from all gifts received on the fifty pages by the close, down by
the gifts refunded or charged back, up by offline sponsorship banked by the close and by
employer matching confirmed by the close, to the team's raised total; a chart counting the
places under each outcome; and a runner table (place type, result, minimum, raised total,
Gift Aid, outcome, amount due) that sorts when a column header is clicked and narrows to
what is typed in a search box. The dossier, written in Markdown, gives every outcome its
own section listing the runners in it with the record that decided each one, and deals
with every waiver, deferral request, refund, employer match, consent change and change to
the donations that affected anyone. The reconciliation sheet is a CSV with a row per
runner: runner id, place type, result, minimum, gifts by the close net of refunds,
offline banked, employer match, raised total, eligible Gift Aid gifts and their total,
Gift Aid, outcome, amount due and notice route. Any file names will do as long as the
dossier's contains the word dossier and the sheet's the word reconciliation. Open each
file again after saving it and read it through.

To finish, write me a note able to stand up to questions, and make it your final reply:
the whole note there, not a pointer to an email or to the files. I need the team's raised total
and every waterfall step with its amount; the Gift Aid claimed and the number of claim
lines; the count of places under each outcome; each invoice with its runner and amount,
and the invoice total. Say how many place records you corrected and how many you left
as they were. Tell me which deferral requests stood and which did not, which waivers
stood and which did not and why, which refunds, employer matches and other changes to the
donations changed a total, and which runners were emailed rather than texted and why. Tell
me who wins the Kestrel Shield: it goes to the highest raised total among the runners who
started, and is shared when totals are equal. Go through each view put forward in the team
chat about the close-out and tell me if it holds and on which paper, and name every paper
in the folders that turned out not to matter.
