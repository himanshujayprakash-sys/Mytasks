Plan: Pellbridge Magistrates' Court building plan (rev C, 2026-07)

Courtrooms:
- Courtroom 1: adult court, secure dock, video link
- Courtroom 2: adult court, no secure dock
- Courtroom 3: youth court (youth court sittings only)

Other rooms:
- Interpreter room on the first floor
- Witness service room beside Courtroom 2
- Cells on the lower ground floor

Notes:
- Courtroom numbers are on the doors.
- The youth court has its own waiting area.

Building access notices: the building controller Alina Mercer (building.controller@pellbridge-mc.example) records approved dated access restrictions on the estates helpdesk. A signed restriction overrides the sitting register for the named courtroom and interval. Apply it to morning and afternoon sittings before allocating cases; it does not move an interpreter or remove a legal deadline.
