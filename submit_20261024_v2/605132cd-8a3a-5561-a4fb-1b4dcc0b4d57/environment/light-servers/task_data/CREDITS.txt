About the Wexcombe allotment papers (task 16).

Wexcombe District Council, its three sites, the plots, applicants, households, rents and both policy editions are made up
for this exercise. Councils really do let allotments from waiting lists; the GOV.UK page named here was read for that
background only. None of its wording or numbers appear in the papers.

- "Rent an allotment", GOV.UK, https://www.gov.uk/apply-allotment (the page carries no publication date). Crown
