# Thornwick Juniors: closing the winter registration window

Bethan Quenby, registration secretary at Thornwick Juniors FC. Today is Wednesday 25 November 2026 and it has just gone three.
The Merrowdale & District Youth League shuts its winter registration window at midday tomorrow, and I am sitting on a tribunal
panel all day, so the window for our three league teams (U12, U14 and U16) is in your hands this afternoon.

Four pieces of work make up the window. Every coach named for those teams gets a safeguarding result, and each team is either
cleared or not. Every row the team managers drafted gets its final status in our squad registrations table, which the league
reads when the window shuts. The families of the children we register are invoiced for what they still owe for 2026-27. And each
family we invoice is reminded once.

Nobody gets to decide any of this by feel. The registration handbook fixes the order in which a player's status is worked out, the
wording of every status, how a team is cleared and how fees, invoices and reminders are done; the safeguarding policy decides who may
be named as a coach; the fee schedule holds the fees; and the league's rules, in the form they take for this window, set the squad
limits. Follow them exactly, together with whatever records they send you to. The shelf also holds papers that are no longer current,
and telling the live ones from the dead ones is part of the work, so I will leave that to you.

## What is available

Our papers are in the data folder of your workspace. You can also use the registration base (members, squad registrations, coaches,
team clearance, families and last season's registrations, plus the league portal's own tables), the registration desk mailbox, our
two text numbers, the club WhatsApp line, the club helpdesk, the volunteer register, the fixtures calendar, the renewal form, the club
accounts and the committee chat. Other club systems are reachable but hold nothing for the window.

Plenty of people have opinions about the squads, in the chat and by mail, the chair among them. An opinion is not a record; only the
handbook or the policy can make something one. If a view from anybody, mine included, conflicts with those papers and the records
they rely on, go with the papers, and say which one decided it.

## The work

1. Put a safeguarding result on the row of every coach named for the three teams, and fill in each team's clearance row with its
   coaches, using the handbook's wording.
2. Give every drafted row in the squad registrations table its status, using the handbook's wording.
3. Raise the 2026-27 membership invoices in the club accounts in the way the handbook describes: one per family.
4. Remind each invoiced family, by text from the fees line or by email from the registration desk, as the handbook directs.

## Three files

Once the records are done, save three files at the top level of your workspace (not in a subfolder), and open each one again after saving
it to see what it says.

First, an offline web page that carries all of its own content: no outside links or scripts. At the head, in big type, the total of
players registered and the total invoiced, with each team's registered count next to them. Under that, a chart showing every drafted
player's status team by team; a sortable, filterable table of all squad rows (player, team, years at the club, status), with a
filter box; and the family invoices with their totals.

Second, a CSV called something with "fees" in it, holding a line for each registered player: player, team, family, fee, sibling
discount, hardship (yes or no), deposit, balance and invoice reference.

Third, a Markdown safeguarding dossier, with "safeguarding" in its name: one section per coach checked (their result and the record
behind it) and one per team (clearance and its two coaches).

## Limits

- Do not text any family that has told us to stop texting them.
- The only things you may change are: the safeguarding results of the three teams' coaches, the three teams' clearance rows, the
  status of the drafted squad rows, the membership invoices, the reminder texts from the fees line and the reminder emails to invoiced
  families.
- Leave alone the committee chat, the WhatsApp line, the helpdesk, the volunteer register, the fixtures calendar, the renewal form,
  the league portal's tables, the members and families tables and every other system, and send no other mail.

## Report back

Finish with a note for the committee, written out in full in your closing message itself rather than pointing to an earlier message
or a file. It should give the number registered overall and per team; each coach's result and the record
it rests on; each team's clearance and coaches; each team's squad limit and its source; every withdrawn, refused or held player with
the reason, and by name any players held at a tie on a squad limit; how many invoices you raised and their total, with the fee,
discount, deposit and hardship rules used; and how many reminders were texts and how many emails, which families got an email
instead of a text and why. Take each claim made in the committee chat about the window and say whether it holds and which paper
settles it, and name the papers that turned out not to matter.

## What decides it

The coaches and team clearance tables, the squad registrations table, the invoices, the reminders and the three files are what I
will examine; they decide whether the window is closed properly. I will read the note on its own for how well it explains the
decisions, but good writing will not rescue a wrong status or a wrong sum.
