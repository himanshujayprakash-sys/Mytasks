Thornbeck Homes - working days 2026

The office, the contact centre and Direct Works work Monday to Friday. The out-of-hours service covers evenings, weekends and bank holidays.

Bank holidays in England, 2026:
Thursday 1 January - New Year's Day
Friday 3 April - Good Friday
Monday 6 April - Easter Monday
Monday 4 May - Early May bank holiday
Monday 25 May - Spring bank holiday
Monday 31 August - Summer bank holiday
Friday 25 December - Christmas Day
Monday 28 December - Boxing Day (substitute day)

There is no bank holiday in September, October or November 2026.
