# Repairs performance reporting note

Agreed with the Director of Operations, January 2026.

Each month, once the compensation run is done, the Repairs Performance Officer posts the month's figures in the repairs performance channel:
the total compensation awarded, the number of awards, the total recharged to contractors, and the worst contractor of the month.

The worst contractor is the external contractor with the most appointments counted as missed in that month's run. Direct Works is our own
team and is reported separately in the Direct Works report, not in this measure. If two or more contractors share the highest number, name each
of them.
