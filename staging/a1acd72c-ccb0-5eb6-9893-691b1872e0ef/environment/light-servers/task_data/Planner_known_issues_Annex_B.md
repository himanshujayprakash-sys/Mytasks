# Annex B to the Repairs Service Standard - planner known issues

Keep this annex with the standard. It records how the planner's appointment outcomes can mislead, and what governs instead.

## B1. Direct Works appointments marked attended automatically
The planner marks every Direct Works appointment "Attended" at 18:00 on the day unless the operative raises an exception from their handset.
An operative who is on leave or off sick that day does not raise one, so the appointment shows as attended although nobody went.
Where the operative booked for a Direct Works appointment had approved leave or recorded sickness on that day in the HR system, the
appointment did not take place: it is a missed appointment (standard s.6.1).

## B2. Contractor portal outcomes
Contractors key outcomes from their own app. A tenant who rings us to cancel can still appear on the contractor's side as a no-show. The contact
centre's note on the repair ticket governs (standard s.9.2).

## B3. SLA dashboard export
The dashboard export works from the dates keyed into the planner and the outcome codes as keyed. It is a planning view and is not used to
decide compensation.

## B4. Planner downtime
The planner was offline for its update on Saturday 31 October 2026 between 07:00 and 11:00. No records were lost.
