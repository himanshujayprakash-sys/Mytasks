#### Summary implications for task design (advisory)

- Grade from sidecar-side evidence collected through service-scoped artifacts and sidecar collect hooks into a separate verifier with pre-baked dependencies; never from files, logs or transcripts in main.
- Assume every solver can bypass the MCP client and speak raw HTTP or JSON-RPC to any sidecar it can reach, and that Inspector or SDK-based enumeration exposes every tool, resource and prompt.
- Keep answer material out of the agent image entirely, including scaffold auto-load paths (AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, .mcp.json, skills dirs), git history, and anything reachable via file:// or fetch.
- Confirm on Harbor 0.23.0 locally how no-network and allowlist interact with compose sidecars before choosing network_mode for an MCP task.
